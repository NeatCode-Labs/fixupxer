# FixupXer: Clean URLs, Enhanced Privacy

## Take Control of Your Links

FixupXer is an elegant, privacy-focused Android app that gives you control over the links you share. Strip away invasive tracking parameters, improve social media links, and share cleaner URLs with one tap.

## Why FixupXer?

In today's digital world, the links we share often contain hidden tracking parameters that compromise privacy. Social media platforms embed user data in URLs, and companies track your every click. FixupXer puts you back in control.

## Key Benefits

### 🛡️ Enhanced Privacy

Remove invasive tracking parameters from URLs automatically. FixupXer identifies and strips away tracking elements like UTM parameters, Facebook click IDs, and dozens of other tracking codes that follow your digital footprint.

### 🔄 Better Social Media Sharing

Transform your Twitter/X and Instagram links into better formats:
- Twitter/X links become fixupx.com links for better embedding and viewing
- Instagram links convert to kkinstagram.com for improved privacy and functionality

### ⚡ Seamless Experience

Designed for simplicity and speed:
- Process URLs with a single tap
- Share directly from any app via Android's share menu
- Copy, share, or open cleaned links instantly

### 🔒 Privacy-First Design

- **Zero Data Collection**: Your data never leaves your device
- **No Internet Required**: Process links locally without sending them to any server
- **No Permissions**: FixupXer requires no special permissions to function
