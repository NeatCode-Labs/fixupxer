Bug-fix release:

* Links opened from the Reddit app no longer land on a "reddit.com/invalid_token" error page: the out.reddit.com destination is now extracted instead of having its functional parameters stripped.
* Redirect-wrapper handling is now host-agnostic (Reddit, Facebook, LinkedIn, newsletters, ...), not just Google/Gmail.
* Fixed valid single URLs being wrongly rejected on slower devices (validator regexes are now precompiled).
* 9 new regression tests.
