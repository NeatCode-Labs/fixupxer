FixupXer is a privacy-focused Android app that enhances your URL sharing experience by removing tracking parameters and improving social media embeds.

<b>Features:</b>

• <b>Privacy First:</b> Removes tracking parameters like UTM codes, fbclid, and more
• <b>Better Twitter/X Embeds:</b> Converts Twitter/X URLs to fixupx.com for improved Discord/Telegram embeds
• <b>Enhanced Instagram Links:</b> Converts Instagram URLs to kkinstagram.com for better viewing
• <b>No Internet Permission:</b> All processing happens locally on your device
• <b>Quick Sharing:</b> Process URLs via the share menu from any app
• <b>Clipboard Support:</b> Copy cleaned URLs with one tap

<b>How to Use:</b>

1. Share any URL to FixupXer from your browser or any app
2. The URL is automatically cleaned and enhanced
3. Share the processed URL or copy it to clipboard

You can also paste URLs directly in the app for processing.

<b>Privacy:</b>

• No data collection or analytics
• No internet permission required
• All URL processing happens locally
• Open source for transparency

<b>Note:</b> This app uses third-party services (fixupx.com and kkinstagram.com) to enhance social media embeds. These services are not operated by us and may change without notice.
