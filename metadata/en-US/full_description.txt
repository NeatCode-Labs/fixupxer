FixupXer is a privacy-focused Android app that enhances your URL sharing experience by removing tracking parameters and improving social media embeds. With a revolutionary modular engine, FixupXer removes 964 unique tracking parameters across 25+ platforms.

<b>🚀 Key Features:</b>

• <b>Privacy Champion:</b> Industry-leading removal of 964 tracking parameters across all major platforms
• <b>Modular Architecture:</b> 11 specialized cleaners for optimal performance on each platform
• <b>Conversion History:</b> Keep track of your URL conversions with local history (privacy-first, never leaves your device)
• <b>Better Social Media Embeds:</b> Convert Twitter/X, Instagram, and Facebook URLs for improved embeds
• <b>Lightning Fast:</b> 5x faster processing with smart caching and parallel cleaning
• <b>100% Offline:</b> No internet permission required - all processing happens locally
• <b>Android 15 Ready:</b> Full edge-to-edge support with modern Material Design 3

<b>📱 Platform Coverage:</b>

• <b>Social Media:</b> Facebook (119 params), Instagram (67), Twitter/X (99), TikTok (124), LinkedIn (117), Reddit (91)
• <b>E-commerce:</b> Amazon (147 params), AliExpress (100+)
• <b>Content:</b> YouTube (139 params), Substack (87)
• <b>Search:</b> Google Search with URL extraction (140 params)
• <b>Universal:</b> General tracking removal for any website (106 params)

<b>✨ How to Use:</b>

1. Share any URL to FixupXer from your browser or any app
2. The URL is automatically cleaned and enhanced
3. Choose to share, open, or copy the processed URL
4. Check your conversion history anytime (optional feature)

You can also paste URLs directly in the app for processing.

<b>🔒 Privacy & Security:</b>
