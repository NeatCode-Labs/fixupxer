FixupXer removes tracking from URLs before you copy, share, or open them. Processing runs entirely on your device, with no ads, account, analytics, or Android permissions.

<b>Clean and improve links</b>

• Remove known tracking parameters from YouTube, Instagram, X, TikTok, Facebook, Amazon, Google, Reddit, and many other services. A universal cleaner covers other websites.
• Keep unknown and functional parameters by default. Instagram post/Reel links keep a valid carousel image selector; individual Story permalinks lose query parameters and fragments.
• Unwrap supported redirect links entirely offline.
• Optionally convert links to embed-friendly frontends for chat previews or readers that can display public content without an account. Choose built-in options or add custom domains in Settings.

<b>Use it your way</b>

• Share a URL to FixupXer, paste it into the app, or select text and choose "Clean link" where the source app supports it.
• Choose the next action: open in an app or browser, share, or copy.
• Enable Browser Mode in Settings and select FixupXer as Android's default browser to process eligible links automatically. Verified App Links may bypass it.
• Keep Browser frontend choices separate from Main/Share choices. Remember a preferred browser or an app choice for a website host.
• Native actions restore supported X, Instagram, and TikTok embed links to the original platform. Browser, Share, and Copy keep the processed URL; failed actions offer Retry.

<b>Private Link Guard</b>

Get an offline warning when a link still contains credentials, email addresses, auth tokens, or precise coordinates. Review the finding before sharing. Remove a flagged query parameter when available, or go back. Sensitive originals stay out of history and cache; fully cleaned results may be saved as redacted entries when History is on. The guard cannot detect every private value.

<b>Custom rules and local controls</b>

• Create optional rules for hosts or URL patterns, with ordered processing phases, templates, and Teach-from-example drafts.
• Preview changes in Test Lab, save per-rule test cases, and import or export rule bundles. Custom rules start disabled.
• Use optional local history with Undo, manual settings and rule backups, and System, Light, or Dark themes.
• Open online Help for setup, examples, and troubleshooting.

<b>Privacy and third-party services</b>

FixupXer makes no network requests. Opening or sharing a result hands it to another app or service with its own privacy policy. Optional frontends are independently operated and may change or stop working; conversion can be disabled.
